FAQ

Frequently asked questions about lead engagement

What is lead engagement?

Lead engagement is the process of communicating with potential customers, answering their questions, understanding their needs, and guiding them toward the next step in their buying journey.

What is an omnichannel lead engagement platform?

An omnichannel lead engagement platform brings customer conversations from multiple channels into a connected workspace, helping teams manage interactions, maintain context, and deliver consistent responses.

Can I manage WhatsApp and Instagram messages in one inbox?

Chatberry is designed to bring supported WhatsApp and Instagram conversations into a shared workspace, allowing your team to respond and collaborate from one place.

Can website chat and form submissions be connected to lead engagement?

Website chat can provide a direct conversation channel, while form submissions can feed lead information into your configured workflow. The exact process depends on the integration and setup.

Can multiple team members work on the same customer conversation?

A shared workspace can help relevant team members and departments collaborate on customer enquiries, with conversation ownership and internal coordination.

How does AI help with lead engagement?

AI can assist with answering common questions, drafting relevant replies, using connected business knowledge, and supporting configured workflows. Human team members can handle conversations that require additional judgement or expertise.

How does lead engagement help improve conversions?

Timely responses, clear ownership, relevant answers, and consistent follow-ups can reduce missed opportunities and help potential customers make informed decisions. Actual conversion improvements depend on the business, its processes, and lead quality.

What is the difference between lead engagement and lead management?

Lead engagement focuses on communicating with and building relationships with potential customers. Lead management focuses on organizing, tracking, assigning, and progressing leads through the sales process. The two work best together.
