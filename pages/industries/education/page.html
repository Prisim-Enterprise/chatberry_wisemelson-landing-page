What can Chatberry do for educational institutions?

Chatberry helps institutions manage student enquiries, admissions, communication, marketing campaigns, support requests, staff workflows, and analytics through connected tools and automations.

Can Chatberry automate the admission process?

Chatberry can automate supported stages such as enquiry capture, lead qualification, counselling bookings, follow-ups, application reminders, and admission notifications. The exact workflow depends on the institution’s process and integrations.

Can AI answer questions about our courses and fees?

AI Agents can use configured institutional knowledge, such as course details, FAQs, prospectuses, and fee information, to answer supported questions. Sensitive or complex cases can be routed to staff.

Can we manage multiple admission counsellors and departments?

Staff and Access Management can organise users, departments, roles, permissions, and responsibility for conversations or tickets.

Can we track which marketing campaigns generate admissions?

Chatberry can track campaign engagement and configured conversion events. Connecting lead sources and admission records helps provide a clearer picture of the journey from campaign to enrolment.

Does Chatberry provide student and team analytics?

Relevant analytics can cover campaign performance, admission journeys, response times, ticket resolution, workload, and other available operational metrics. The depth of reporting depends on the data and integrations configured.

Can Chatberry send fee reminders and payment updates?

Scheduled reminders and supported payment workflows can be configured. Payment confirmations and account-specific updates require access to the relevant payment or institutional records.

Can we integrate our existing education systems?

Chatberry supports integrations and can use available APIs and webhooks to connect suitable systems. Specific compatibility depends on the system and the integration method it offers.

Is Chatberry suitable for both online and offline institutions?

Schools, colleges, coaching institutes, training centres, and online education businesses can configure workflows suited to their operations.

Can students contact the institution outside office hours?

AI Agents can handle supported routine questions at any time when the relevant services are configured. Queries that require human judgement or staff action can be routed for follow-up.

Can we manage parent complaints and student support requests?

Ticketing can categorise, assign, track, and escalate requests, helping teams manage cases through to resolution.

Can Chatberry help reduce admission drop-offs?

Chatberry can support timely follow-ups, application reminders, counselling bookings, and conversion tracking. These workflows help institutions identify and address drop-off points, although results depend on implementation and student response.
