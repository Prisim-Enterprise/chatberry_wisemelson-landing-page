FAQ

Analytics FAQs

What is business analytics software?

Business analytics software helps businesses review performance data, identify trends, and make more informed decisions. The specific data available depends on the systems and events connected to the platform.

What can I track with Chatberry Analytics?

Chatberry Analytics brings together the campaign, customer engagement, team and workflow metrics your Chatberry setup records, such as campaign delivery, reads and replies, conversation volumes, response times and ticket progress. The exact metrics available depend on the channels, features and integrations you use.

Can I measure WhatsApp campaign performance?

Yes. You can review the WhatsApp campaign metrics Chatberry records, such as messages sent, delivered, read and replied to, where supported. Other measures, like link opens, appear only when tracking for them is set up.

Can analytics help me understand customer behaviour?

Analytics can help you review recorded customer interactions and engagement patterns. How far the journey analysis goes depends on which customer events and integrations are available in your setup.

How does AI help with business analytics?

AI-assisted insights can help surface noteworthy patterns, summarise changes, and suggest areas to investigate. Insights are based on your available data and keep what the data shows separate from possible explanations.

Can AI tell me exactly why campaign performance dropped?

Not necessarily. Data can show where performance changed, but the cause may need further investigation. Possible factors are presented as things to check, not as confirmed explanations, unless the evidence clearly establishes one.

Can I understand team and workflow performance?

Yes, where your Chatberry setup records the relevant team, ticket or workflow events. Analytics can then help you review progress, response times and delays across departments and workflows. It reports on how work moves, not on monitoring individual activity.

How is Analytics different from Team Collaboration?

Team Collaboration helps teams coordinate work and understand responsibilities. Analytics focuses on performance trends, engagement, and operational outcomes so businesses can see what is changing and where to focus. See Team Collaboration
