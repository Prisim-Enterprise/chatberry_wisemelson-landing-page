What is an AI knowledge base?

An AI knowledge base is an organised source of information that connected AI systems can use to answer questions and support tasks. Chatberry helps businesses organise documents, website content, FAQs, and structured data for use across supported AI experiences and workflows.

How can I train AI with my business documents?

Upload supported documents and organise the relevant information in your knowledge base. Connect that knowledge to the AI capabilities you want to use so they can reference available business information.

Can I add website content and structured data?

Yes. Website content can be imported through supported ingestion capabilities, while structured business information can be organised in tables and collections.

Can I manage customer and CRM records?

The Knowledge Base can organise structured customer and business records where supported. CRM integrations and workflows can use relevant information according to their configuration and permissions.

Can I automate payment and subscription reminders?

Payment or subscription data from a supported source can trigger reminders through configured workflows, provided the required data, conditions, and messaging channel are available.

Can AI learn from customer interactions?

Available interaction patterns can help identify recurring questions and knowledge gaps. How these insights are collected and incorporated depends on the configured product capabilities.

Does the Knowledge Base update automatically?

New information can be added and existing information updated through the supported knowledge management process. Automatic updates depend on the specific ingestion and workflow capabilities enabled in the setup.

Can multiple AI agents use the same knowledge?

Business knowledge can be made available to multiple connected AI experiences where the relevant integrations, permissions, and configurations are supported.

What types of information can I add?

Supported sources may include documents, website content, FAQs, and structured data. Available formats and ingestion options depend on the platform’s supported capabilities.
