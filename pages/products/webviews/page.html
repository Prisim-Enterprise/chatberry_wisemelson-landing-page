What is Chatberry Webviews?

Chatberry Webviews lets businesses create customer-facing web experiences such as landing pages, forms, booking journeys, and other custom pages supported by the platform.

Can I create a page using AI?

Yes. Webviews supports an AI-assisted creation experience that helps turn a description of what you need into a starting point you can review and customise.

Do I need to start from scratch?

No. You can start by describing the experience you want AI to help create, then refine the result to fit your needs.

Can I customise the page AI creates?

Yes. You can refine the generated experience with the editing tools available in Webviews, such as its content, fields and steps. The exact options depend on the builder features in your setup.

Can I publish directly from Chatberry?

Yes. You can publish an experience from within Chatberry, and it becomes available to customers through its live link.

What kinds of experiences can I create?

Depending on the capabilities available in your setup, you can create landing pages, forms, registration experiences, booking journeys, and custom mini-app-like pages.

Can customers access my published page through a link?

Yes. A published experience is available through its live link, which you can share with customers, for example in a WhatsApp message. Other sharing options depend on your setup.

Can I see how visitors use my page?

Where tracking is supported and configured, you can examine relevant visitor interactions and completion activity to help understand how people use the experience.

Can I update a page after publishing?

You can go back to the builder and make changes to your experience. How and when those changes reach the live page depends on the publishing options in your setup.

Do I need coding skills to use Webviews?

No coding is needed to create a page with AI and refine it with the builder’s editing tools. The availability of more advanced customisation depends on your setup.
