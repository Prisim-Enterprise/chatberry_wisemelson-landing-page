Behaviour-based nurturing

Let lead behaviour
guide the next step.

A lead’s actions can tell you what they’re interested in. Use available engagement signals to make follow-ups more relevant instead of sending every lead through the same sequence.

  • Clicked a linkFollow up with related information or a relevant next step, when click tracking is available.
  • Replied to a messageMove the lead towards a conversation or sales handover instead of continuing the sequence.
  • Submitted a formContinue with information relevant to the request.
  • Showed interest in an offerShare the next piece of information that helps the lead evaluate it.
  • Stopped engagingChange the approach or move the lead into a longer-term sequence, where rules are configured.

Signals depend on the tracking you have set up. Opt-outs, consent and messaging policies are always respected. Continue the conversation across channels

FAQ

Lead Nurturing FAQs

What is lead nurturing?

Lead nurturing is the process of maintaining relevant communication with potential customers over time, helping them understand an offering and move towards a purchase when they’re ready.

What is lead nurturing software?

Lead nurturing software helps businesses organise follow-ups and deliver planned or behaviour-based communications to prospects throughout their buying journey.

How does automated lead nurturing work?

A business defines a sequence of messages, timing rules, and, where supported, conditions based on lead activity. The journey can guide leads through relevant follow-ups without requiring every message to be sent manually.

What is the difference between lead nurturing and lead management?

Lead management organises lead records, ownership, and pipeline progress. Lead nurturing focuses on maintaining interest and guiding prospects towards the next stage through relevant communication.

Can lead nurturing messages change based on behaviour?

Yes, when the platform supports behaviour tracking and conditional workflows. For example, a lead who responds may follow a different path from someone who has not engaged.

How often should businesses follow up with leads?

The appropriate frequency depends on the audience, offer, buying cycle, and previous engagement. Day 3, 5, 7, 9, and 15 can be an example, but businesses should adjust timing and stop or change follow-ups when a lead responds, opts out, or converts.

What content should a lead nurturing sequence include?

Useful content may include educational information, answers to common questions, product details, relevant proof points, invitations to book a demo, and gentle re-engagement messages. The sequence should reflect the lead’s stage and interests.

How can businesses measure lead nurturing performance?

Depending on available tracking, businesses can review delivery, reads, replies, clicks, re-engagement, stage drop-off, and attributed conversions. Metrics should be interpreted in the context of the campaign and buying journey.
