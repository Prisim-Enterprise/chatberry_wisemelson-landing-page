What is Workflow Orchestration?

Workflow orchestration connects business events, logic, data, people, systems and actions into one coordinated process.

What can I build with Chatberry?

You can combine internal nodes, business logic, AI, data, conversations, APIs, webhooks and external systems to create workflows around your business processes.

Does Chatberry support different types of workflow nodes?

Yes. Chatberry can provide different node types for communication, logic, data, customer experiences, integrations and other business actions.

Can I connect external applications?

Yes. Workflows can connect with external applications and systems through available integrations, APIs and webhooks.

Can I use Facebook Leads as a workflow trigger?

Supported lead sources can trigger workflows, allowing incoming leads to be processed, qualified, routed and acted on automatically.

Can AI create workflows?

Yes. You can describe a process in natural language and AI can generate a workflow that you can review and customize.

Can I add business logic?

Yes. Workflows can use conditions, intent, queries, variables, conversation state and other logic to determine what happens next.

Can people be part of an automated workflow?

Yes. Workflows can pause for approvals, assignments or human actions before continuing automatically.

Can workflows interact with data?

Yes. Workflow nodes can work with configured data and queries to retrieve or use information during execution.

Can I monitor workflows?

Yes. You can monitor workflow execution and identify processes that are running, waiting, completed, failed or require attention.
