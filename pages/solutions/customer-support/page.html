FAQ

Customer Support FAQs

Need to track requests through to resolution?

See Ticket Management

What is AI-powered customer support?

AI-powered customer support uses artificial intelligence to answer common questions, understand customer requests, and guide conversations. It can work alongside human agents to handle requests that need personal assistance.

Can Chatberry AI transfer a conversation to a human agent?

AI-to-human handoff can be configured where supported, allowing a conversation to be handled by a human agent when further assistance is needed.

Can multiple agents manage customer conversations?

A shared inbox and team management features can help multiple agents manage conversations, assign ownership, and transfer chats according to the team’s workflow.

Can AI answer questions using my business knowledge?

Chatberry can use supported business knowledge sources, such as documents and website content, to help AI answer customer questions. Answer quality depends on the information available and how the system is configured.

Can conversations be routed to different departments?

Conversations can be routed to appropriate teams based on the available assignment and routing rules.

Can I track customer support performance?

Support analytics can help teams review available metrics such as response times, conversation volume, unresolved conversations, and agent activity.

Which channels can I manage through Chatberry?

Chatberry supports customer engagement through configured channels. The channels you can manage depend on the integrations and capabilities available in your Chatberry setup.

How is Customer Support different from Ticket Management?

Customer Support focuses on conversations, answering questions, assisting customers, and coordinating human and AI support. Ticket Management focuses on recording requests, assigning responsibility, tracking status, and managing issues through resolution.
