FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Content Engine

What is Chatberry Content Engine?

Chatberry Content Engine helps businesses use available business information to create reusable templates, customer replies, campaign messages, and other content for customer communication.

What information can it learn from?

Depending on the setup, it can use documents, website information, connected business data, and eligible customer conversation information.

Can it create WhatsApp templates?

It can help prepare reusable WhatsApp message content. Templates intended for WhatsApp Business Platform use must follow applicable template approval and messaging requirements.

Can it help customer support teams reply faster?

It can help prepare reusable replies and support context-aware responses using available business knowledge and supported workflows.

Can AI use this knowledge to answer customers?

Where AI replies are enabled, available business knowledge can provide context for responses. Accuracy depends on the quality, relevance, and freshness of the available information.

Does it automatically learn whenever business data changes?

This depends on the connected sources and update mechanisms available. Automatic synchronisation or continuous learning should only be claimed for sources and workflows that support it.

Can the content be used in campaigns and workflows?

Content can support campaigns and configured workflows where the relevant Chatberry features and integrations are available.
