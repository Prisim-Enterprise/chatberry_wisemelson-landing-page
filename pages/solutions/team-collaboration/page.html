What is team collaboration software?

Team collaboration software helps employees communicate, coordinate tasks, share updates and manage work together. Chatberry Team Collaboration brings internal communication, task ownership, recorded time and team activity into one organised workspace.

Can managers track team activity in Chatberry?

Managers can review activity summaries, recent task updates, completed work and pending tasks for the teams they manage. What appears depends on how your team uses tasks and time entries in your setup.

Can Chatberry track the time spent by team members?

Where time tracking is enabled in your setup, team members can record time against tasks and activities, and managers can review daily and weekly summaries of that recorded time. The figures come from recorded time entries, not from screen or keystroke monitoring.

Can managers see what tasks each team member has completed?

Where your team tracks work as tasks, managers can review each person's assignments, current statuses, completion history and outstanding work.

Can I control what each team member can access?

Access is managed through roles, such as owner, manager and team member, and can be organised by department where supported. The exact permissions available depend on your plan and setup.

Does Chatberry support internal team chat?

Team Collaboration includes internal chat for discussing work, sharing updates and coordinating tasks with teammates. Where supported in your setup, a conversation can be connected to the related task so the context stays together.

Can teams manage work across departments?

Teams can hand work from one department to another with a clear owner, status and next action at each step. How handoffs run depends on the task and workflow setup in your workspace.

How is Team Collaboration different from Customer Support?

Customer Support focuses on conversations between your business and its customers. Team Collaboration focuses on internal communication, task coordination, team activity and accountability.
