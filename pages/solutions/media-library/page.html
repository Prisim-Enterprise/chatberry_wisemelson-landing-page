What is Chatberry Media Library?

Chatberry Media Library is a central place to save, manage, and reuse business media, including images, videos, and documents.

Can I generate images inside Chatberry?

Yes. You can use AI to generate images directly inside Chatberry and save them to your Media Library for future use.

Can I reuse media without uploading it every time?

Yes. Save your media once and access it again whenever you need it.

What types of files can I store?

You can store business media such as images, videos, and documents, subject to supported file formats and size limits.

Can I use my saved media in campaigns?

Your saved media can be reused in the Chatberry features that support media selection, helping you avoid repeatedly uploading the same assets.

Can I use AI-generated images later?

Yes. Save generated images to your Media Library so you can access and reuse them whenever needed.
