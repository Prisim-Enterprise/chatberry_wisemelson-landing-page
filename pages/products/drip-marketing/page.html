What is drip marketing?

Drip marketing is a method of sending a sequence of messages over time based on triggers, schedules, customer actions, or defined conditions.

How is AI-powered drip marketing different from traditional drip campaigns?

Traditional drip campaigns commonly follow predetermined schedules. AI-powered drip marketing can use available customer behaviour and engagement patterns to help determine more relevant follow-ups and improve journey decisions.

Can Chatberry change a sequence based on customer behaviour?

Yes. Chatberry drip journeys can branch based on customer behaviour and configured conditions. Available branches depend on the signals and actions supported by the configured journey.

What does AI do in Chatberry Drip Marketing?

AI helps interpret available customer interactions and behaviour patterns, support more relevant follow-ups, and identify opportunities to improve customer journeys.

What analytics can businesses track?

Depending on integrations and tracking configuration, businesses can monitor message delivery, reads, replies, link clicks, journey progression, drop-offs, branch performance, and tracked conversions.

Can I create different messages for different customer segments?

Customer segments and behaviour-based conditions can be used to deliver more relevant messages to different audiences, depending on the available data and configured journey logic.

Can I automate follow-ups without sending every message manually?

Yes. Once a sequence and its conditions are configured, supported messages and follow-up actions can run automatically according to the journey rules.

How is drip marketing different from campaigns?

Campaigns are commonly used to send broadcasts or scheduled messages to an audience. Drip marketing focuses on a sequence of messages over time, often using customer actions and conditions to determine the next step. See Campaigns.
