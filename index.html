    06 / Questions FAQ

    Goodquestions.

    What is Chatberry?

    Chatberry is a customer communication platform. It brings your WhatsApp, Instagram and website chats into one inbox, adds AI agents that reply for you, and gives you campaigns, ticketing, workflows and reports in the same place.

    Does it work with my existing WhatsApp number?

    Chatberry connects to the official WhatsApp Business Platform. We help you set up your number and walk you through what changes, so you can start without guesswork.

    Can my whole team use one number?

    Yes. Everyone works from the same shared inbox, with chats assigned to the right person and internal notes that customers never see.

    Will the AI reply on its own?

    Only as much as you want it to. You decide what the AI agent handles, what it knows about your business, and when it hands a chat over to a person.

    Can it connect to the tools I already use?

    Chatberry connects to common tools such as Google Sheets and payment gateways, and supports webhooks and an API for anything custom. Tell us what you use and we’ll show you how it fits.

    How do I get started?

    Create your workspace at app.prisim.co.in, or message us on WhatsApp and we’ll set up a demo around your business.
