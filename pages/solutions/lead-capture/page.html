FAQ

Frequently asked questions about lead capture

What is lead capture software?

Lead capture software helps businesses collect information from potential customers through websites, forms, chatbots, messaging channels, and other entry points.

Which channels can Chatberry capture leads from?

Chatberry supports lead capture through websites, chatbots, QR codes, WhatsApp, Instagram, links, and forms.

Can I capture leads from both online and offline campaigns?

Yes. Online entry points such as websites and links can be combined with QR codes used on physical materials, provided they direct customers to a configured Chatberry capture experience.

How can I track where my leads come from?

Lead-source information and available analytics can help you understand which entry points generate enquiries. The level of tracking depends on how each source is configured.

How does AI help with lead capture?

AI can use available customer information and behavioural context to help interpret interactions and inform relevant next steps. Specific actions depend on the AI capabilities and workflows configured in Chatberry.

How is lead capture different from lead management, qualification and nurturing?

Lead capture collects a potential customer’s details from an entry point. Lead management organizes and tracks those leads. Lead qualification evaluates which leads are most promising. Lead nurturing builds follow-ups that move prospects closer to a decision.

Can I connect lead capture with other Chatberry features?

Lead capture can connect with relevant workflows involving Lead Management, AI Lead Qualification, Lead Nurturing, Campaign Management, and Customer Journeys.
