What types of hospitality businesses can use Chatberry?

Chatberry can support accommodation businesses, restaurants and cafés, travel agencies and tour operators, and recreation or entertainment venues. The workflows depend on each business’s needs and connected systems.

Can Chatberry help turn enquiries into bookings?

Chatberry can help answer questions, share relevant details and booking links, collect enquiry information, route requests, and follow up. Booking confirmation and conversion reporting depend on the booking process and connected data.

How can AI help a hospitality business?

AI can answer common questions using the business’s information, help customers explore options, collect enquiry details, and direct requests to staff when human assistance is needed.

Can we send offers to customers who did not book?

Chatberry can support follow-up campaigns using available customer interaction and connected data. Targeting must respect applicable consent, messaging, and channel requirements.

Can Chatberry help manage travel agents and channel partners?

Chatberry can help teams organise partner enquiries, referrals, follow-ups, and status tracking. Direct integration and data synchronisation depend on the partner’s systems and available connection options.

What can we see in analytics?

Depending on connected channels and systems, teams can review enquiry sources, campaign performance, customer responses, follow-up activity, response times, and booking progress.
