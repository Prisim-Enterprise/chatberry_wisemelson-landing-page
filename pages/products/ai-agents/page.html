What are AI Agents in Chatberry?

AI Agents are business-focused AI workers that can understand requests, use business context, communicate, create workflows, perform actions and involve people when needed.

Can I talk to Chatberry AI using voice?

Yes. Voice can be used to give instructions, ask questions, create tasks and interact with AI using natural language.

Can AI Agents create workflows?

Yes. You can describe the process you want and AI can generate a starting workflow that you can review and customize.

Can AI create templates and campaigns?

Yes. AI can help create templates, campaign structures, follow-up sequences and other reusable business assets.

Can AI analyze my business?

Yes. AI can investigate available business data and answer questions about customers, campaigns, conversations, workload and performance.

Can AI take actions for me?

Yes. Depending on permissions, AI Agents can create tasks, update records, prepare messages, trigger workflows and perform other configured actions.

Can AI work proactively?

Yes. AI can identify configured conditions, pending work, follow-ups and other situations that require attention and surface them to your team.

Can AI work with multiple business modules?

Yes. AI Agents can work with connected Chatberry capabilities such as Customer OS, conversations, workflows, knowledge, tasks, ticketing, campaigns and analytics.

Can I control what the AI is allowed to do?

Yes. Permissions and approval requirements can be defined so sensitive or important actions can require human review.

Can a human take over?

Yes. AI can hand work to the appropriate person along with the relevant conversation, context, history and suggested next action.
