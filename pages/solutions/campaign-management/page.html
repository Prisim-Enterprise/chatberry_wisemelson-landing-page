FAQ

Frequently asked questions about WhatsApp campaigns

What is WhatsApp campaign management software?

WhatsApp campaign management software helps businesses prepare, send, schedule, and measure WhatsApp campaigns for relevant audiences. Chatberry brings campaign execution, follow-ups, and available performance insights into one workflow.

Can I send bulk WhatsApp campaigns to my contact list?

Yes. Chatberry supports campaigns to selected contact lists, subject to the available product capabilities, recipient consent, WhatsApp policies, and applicable template requirements.

How does WhatsApp drip marketing work?

Drip marketing sends a planned sequence of messages at configured intervals. For example, a business can schedule follow-ups for Day 3, Day 5, Day 7, and Day 9 instead of manually sending each message.

Can I retarget customers based on their behaviour?

Behaviour-informed retargeting can use available customer data, supported events, segments, and configured workflows to help plan or trigger relevant follow-ups. The specific events and actions available depend on the connected systems and product setup.

Can Chatberry automatically retry failed WhatsApp messages?

Eligible temporary failures can be retried according to configured rules. Permanent failures, such as invalid numbers or rejected templates, may need correction before another message can be sent.

Can I manage campaigns through ChatGPT or Claude?

Where a supported MCP connection is configured, a compatible AI assistant can use the Chatberry tools and data made available to it. The actions it can perform depend on the exposed tools, permissions, and any required review or confirmation steps.
