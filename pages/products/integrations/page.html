What is an integration in Chatberry?

An integration connects Chatberry with another business system so data and events can move between the two systems and trigger configured actions.

What tools can I connect to Chatberry?

Chatberry can connect with business tools across CRM, commerce, payments, scheduling, productivity, marketing and other categories. Available connectors can expand over time.

Can I connect Shopify to Chatberry?

Yes. Shopify can be connected so supported commerce events and customer information can participate in Chatberry workflows and customer engagement.

Can I connect Zoho to Chatberry?

Yes. Zoho can be connected to bring supported customer and business information into Chatberry workflows and operations.

Can I connect Razorpay or Stripe?

Yes. Payment platforms such as Razorpay and Stripe can be connected so supported payment events can trigger configured actions.

Can I connect Google Sheets?

Yes. Google Sheets can be connected for supported data and workflow use cases.

Can integrations trigger workflows?

Yes. Connected events can trigger configured Chatberry workflows.

What can happen after an integration trigger?

Depending on your workflow, Chatberry can update customer information, send messages, create tasks, create tickets, notify teams, start campaigns, or perform other configured actions.

Can I build a custom integration?

Yes. APIs and webhooks allow developers to connect custom applications and internal systems.

Does Chatberry provide an API?

Yes. Chatberry provides API capabilities for connecting external applications and building custom integrations.

What are webhooks?

Webhooks allow an external system to send event information to Chatberry when something happens. Chatberry can then use that event within configured workflows.

Do I need to replace my existing software?

No. Chatberry is designed to connect with the systems you already use rather than forcing you to replace them.

Can integrations create tasks for my team?

Yes. A connected event can trigger a task and route it through your configured team and workflow structure.

Can integrations trigger customer messages?

Yes. Supported events can trigger configured customer communication and engagement workflows.
