How can Chatberry help real estate businesses?

Chatberry helps real estate teams manage WhatsApp enquiries, collect buyer requirements through webviews, coordinate follow-ups and site visits, organise lead workflows, and review available campaign and sales activity data.

Can we qualify and filter property leads?

You can collect requirements such as budget, location, property type, and purchase timeline, then organise leads using configured categories and rules. Results depend on the information collected and workflow setup.

Can buyers browse properties through WhatsApp?

You can direct buyers from WhatsApp into configured webviews that present property information, filters, enquiry forms, and other supported experiences.

Can AI answer questions about our projects?

AI-assisted experiences can answer questions using approved project information and connected knowledge. Sources, response behaviour, and escalation rules need to be configured appropriately.

Can Chatberry help re-engage older property leads?

Configured workflows and WhatsApp campaigns can support relevant follow-ups for suitable contacts, subject to available data, consent, messaging rules, and trackable events.

Can buyers book site visits through a webview?

A configured webview can collect visit preferences and requested slots. Confirmations and reminders can be connected to the appropriate workflow and availability information.

Can we manage multiple projects and sales teams?

Chatberry can support organised conversations, team assignment, and workflows for different projects and departments, depending on the account and data configuration.

Can we track where property leads drop off?

You can analyse supported campaign, conversation, webview, and sales-stage events. Funnel reporting depends on which events and records are captured or connected.

Can we manage channel partners and brokers?

Configured webviews and workflows can support partner registration, project enquiries, lead submissions, and communication. Commission or inventory functions require the relevant implementation or integration.

Does Chatberry replace our property CRM?

Chatberry can support customer engagement, lead workflows, automation, and connected insights. Whether it replaces existing CRM functions depends on the processes and configuration; integrations may be possible where supported.
