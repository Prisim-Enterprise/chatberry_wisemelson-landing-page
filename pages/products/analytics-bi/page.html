What is Analytics & BI in Chatberry?

Chatberry Analytics & BI helps businesses understand marketing performance, customer interactions, and operational activity through meaningful business data and insights.

What can I track using Chatberry Analytics & BI?

Depending on your configured features and data sources, you can examine campaign delivery, read and reply rates, customer engagement, response times, agent activity, leads, conversions, and business performance metrics.

How is Business Intelligence different from Marketing Intelligence?

Marketing Intelligence focuses on campaign performance, lead generation, engagement, and acquisition. Business Intelligence takes a broader view of business outcomes, such as revenue, growth, and conversion.

What is Operational Intelligence?

Operational Intelligence helps you understand how work gets done across your business. It can include response times, team activity, workload, and workflow performance, depending on the data available.

Can I understand which marketing activities generate customers?

You can analyse campaign and customer engagement data and examine conversion outcomes where the relevant tracking and data integrations are configured. Accurate attribution depends on how those sources are connected.

Can Chatberry help identify customer retention opportunities?

Customer engagement and interaction patterns can help you identify potential follow-up and retention opportunities when the required customer history and activity data are available.

Can insights trigger automated actions?

Insights can inform workflows and follow-up actions through the relevant Chatberry automation capabilities. Automated execution depends on the workflow, triggers, integrations, and permissions configured.

Does Chatberry’s AI learn from business activity?

AI can use available data and feedback to identify patterns and refine recommendations where those capabilities are supported. The exact learning and adaptation behaviour depends on the AI features configured in your Chatberry setup.

Can the AI update business owners about important changes?

Where supported and configured, notifications can help owners stay informed about selected changes, opportunities, risks, and recommended next steps.

Do I need technical expertise to understand the reports?

The goal is to make business information easier to interpret and act on. The level of configuration and technical support required depends on the data sources, reporting needs, and integrations involved.
