What is Chatberry’s Second Brain?

Chatberry’s Second Brain is an AI-powered business memory and action layer that helps people remember tasks, commitments, follow-ups, decisions, and important context. Users can interact with it through conversation and voice to create tasks, ask questions, receive reminders, and take configured actions.

What can I do with the Second Brain?

You can create tasks, set reminders, ask questions, check pending work, update tasks, reschedule actions, capture important information, and interact with business context through natural language or voice.

Can I talk to the Second Brain using voice?

Yes. Users can use voice to create tasks, set reminders, ask questions, check work, and perform supported actions without manually navigating through the interface.

Can the Second Brain remember tasks?

Yes. It can remember tasks and their relevant context, including deadlines, assignments, status, and follow-up requirements.

What happens when a task is not completed?

Depending on the configured workflow, the Second Brain can remind the responsible person, surface overdue work, escalate the task, or help reschedule it.

Can I ask the Second Brain what I need to do?

Yes. You can ask questions such as “What needs my attention today?” or “What’s overdue?” and receive information based on the context available to your Chatberry workspace.

Can the Second Brain create tasks from conversations?

Yes. Users can describe an action naturally, and the Second Brain can create a task with relevant details such as the task description, assignee, timing, and configured workflow.

Can teams use the Second Brain?

Yes. The Second Brain can support individual and team workflows, helping people create, assign, track, and follow up on work.

Is the Second Brain just a task manager?

No. Task management is only one part of it. The Second Brain is designed to remember business context, help users understand what is happening, surface what needs attention, and support configured actions across the business.

Can the Second Brain work with other Chatberry features?

Yes. It can work alongside Customer OS, AI Agents, Workflow Orchestration, Knowledge Base, Conversations, and other connected capabilities within Chatberry.
