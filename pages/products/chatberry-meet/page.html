What is Chatberry Meet?

Chatberry Meet is an online communication and collaboration platform that brings team messaging, voice and video meetings, webinars and virtual engagement together. It helps businesses, educators and professionals connect with teams, customers and audiences.

Can I use Chatberry Meet for online meetings?

Yes. Chatberry Meet supports one-to-one and group meetings, allowing teams, businesses and professionals to connect remotely.

Can external participants join without a Chatberry Meet account?

Participants can join through a browser link or access meetings from within Chatberry Meet, making it convenient to connect with external customers, clients, students and partners.

Can I host webinars using Chatberry Meet?

Yes. Businesses and organisations can host webinars, online presentations, workshops, training sessions and virtual events.

Can schools and coaching centres conduct online classes?

Yes. Educational institutions can use Chatberry Meet for live classes, online coaching, doubt-clearing sessions, guest lectures and parent-teacher meetings.

Can doctors conduct online consultations using Chatberry Meet?

Healthcare professionals can use video meetings to connect with patients remotely, conduct consultations and arrange follow-up discussions. Any healthcare-specific privacy, security and regulatory requirements should be validated for the intended deployment.

Does Chatberry Meet support team communication?

Yes. Chatberry Meet includes channels, direct messages, threads, mentions, saved messages and availability status for organised team communication.
