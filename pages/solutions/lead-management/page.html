FAQ

Lead Management FAQs

What is lead management software?

Lead management software helps businesses organise prospects, assign ownership, track progress through a sales pipeline, and manage the activities needed to convert leads into customers.

What is the difference between lead management and CRM?

Lead management focuses on the journey from prospect to customer, including lead assignment, qualification, follow-ups, and pipeline progress. A CRM can cover the wider customer relationship, including existing customers and ongoing account activity.

Can I manage leads using a Kanban board?

A Kanban-style view groups leads by stage, making it easier to see progress, identify bottlenecks, and move leads through the pipeline when the relevant functionality is available.

Can salespeople view only their own leads?

A salesperson-specific view helps representatives focus on assigned leads and activities, while managers can review team-level progress. Exact visibility depends on the permissions and views supported by Chatberry.

Can I view lead follow-ups in a calendar?

A calendar view can organise scheduled meetings, demos, and follow-ups by date, making upcoming commitments easier to manage.

How can managers identify neglected leads?

Managers can review last activity, upcoming follow-ups, time spent in each stage, and leads without recent engagement. Automated identification depends on the tracking and reporting capabilities implemented in Chatberry.

Can different teams use different sales pipelines?

Separate pipelines can help teams manage different products, services, or sales processes, where multiple pipelines are available in your setup.

What information should a lead record include?

A lead record commonly includes contact details, source, assigned owner, sales stage, activity history, notes, expected value when relevant, and the next follow-up action.
