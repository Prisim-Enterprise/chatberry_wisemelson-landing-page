What are WhatsApp marketing campaigns?

WhatsApp marketing campaigns are planned messages a business sends to customers on WhatsApp, such as offers, product launches, reminders and announcements. They go to people who have agreed to hear from the business, and often include buttons or links so customers can reply, browse or buy without leaving the chat.

How can businesses send bulk WhatsApp messages?

Businesses send bulk WhatsApp messages through tools built on the WhatsApp Business Platform. Marketing messages sent outside an open conversation use pre-approved message templates and should only go to customers who have opted in. A campaign tool like Chatberry lets you choose the template, select the audience and send to many recipients at once.

What is WhatsApp campaign software?

WhatsApp campaign software helps businesses create, schedule, send and measure WhatsApp campaigns from one place. Instead of handling templates, contact lists and results separately, teams build an audience, pick an approved template, choose when to send and review performance in a single workflow.

Can I schedule WhatsApp marketing campaigns in advance?

Yes. In Chatberry you can set a campaign to go out on a future date and time instead of sending it manually. That lets teams prepare promotions ahead of time, line up launches and seasonal offers, and keep a clear view of what is going out next.

How can I track WhatsApp campaign performance?

Track metrics such as messages sent, delivered, read and failed, along with reply rate and opt-outs. Clicks and conversions can be measured where tracking is set up. Comparing these numbers across campaigns shows which messages, offers and audiences perform best, and where delivery or engagement needs attention.

How can AI improve WhatsApp marketing campaigns?

AI can analyse customer behaviour and campaign engagement data to find patterns, such as which offers get more replies or which segments respond fastest. In Chatberry these patterns are shared as insights and recommendations, and your team decides what to change in the next campaign.

Can I launch WhatsApp campaigns through AI assistants using MCP?

Yes, where the relevant tools are enabled. MCP-compatible AI assistants can connect to Chatberry and carry out supported campaign operations, such as preparing a campaign or reviewing results. What an assistant can do depends on your configuration and permissions, and sending can require review and approval first.

How does Chatberry help businesses manage WhatsApp campaigns?

Chatberry brings WhatsApp campaign work into one platform: building audiences, using approved templates, scheduling sends, tracking delivery, reads and replies, and surfacing insights from customer behaviour. Campaigns also connect with Chatberry conversations, workflows and AI, so the replies a campaign earns can be followed up in the same place.
