What is Business Score?

Business Score is Chatberry’s customer behaviour analytics for Webfronts. It helps you understand how visitors interact with your pages, from page views and clicks to form progress, shopping actions and conversions.

What can Business Score track on a Webfront?

Depending on configured events, it can track page views, visitor activity, button clicks, form interactions, product views, cart additions, and conversion events.

Can Business Score track visitors who abandon a form?

Where form-progress events are instrumented, Business Score can help measure form starts, progress, abandonment, and completed submissions. Field-level tracking depends on the implementation.

Can Business Score track add-to-cart events?

Yes, where product and cart events are configured in the Webfront experience. These events help businesses understand product engagement and shopping progression.

Can Business Score track conversions from WhatsApp campaigns?

Business Score can help connect campaign traffic to downstream actions when campaign links, referral information, and relevant conversion events are tracked correctly.

Does Business Score provide AI-powered recommendations?

Business Score can analyse available behavioural data and surface insights and suggested improvements. What it can recommend depends on the AI capabilities enabled and the data available.

Can Business Score compare different campaigns?

Where campaign attribution and event tracking are configured, businesses can compare visitor engagement and downstream conversion performance across campaigns.

Does Business Score only work with Webfronts?

The core use case is analytics across Chatberry Webfronts. Extending tracking to other experiences depends on supported integrations and tracking capabilities.
