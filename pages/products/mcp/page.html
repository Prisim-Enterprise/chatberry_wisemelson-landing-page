What is MCP in Chatberry?

MCP, or Model Context Protocol, provides a standardized way for compatible AI systems to interact with tools and capabilities exposed by Chatberry.

What can AI do with Chatberry through MCP?

Depending on the capabilities and permissions configured, AI can work with customers, conversations, templates, campaigns, workflows, tasks, tickets, knowledge, analytics and other supported Chatberry functions.

Can AI create WhatsApp templates?

Yes. Where template creation is available through the configured MCP capabilities, an AI system can help create templates in Chatberry from a natural-language request.

Can AI create campaigns?

Yes. Compatible AI systems can create or prepare campaigns when the relevant Chatberry campaign capabilities are exposed and authorized.

Can AI create workflows?

Yes. AI can help create workflows using the workflow capabilities made available through Chatberry MCP.

Can AI analyze Chatberry data?

Yes. Compatible AI systems can use available business context and analytics capabilities to answer questions and surface insights.

Can AI create tasks?

Yes. If task creation is exposed through the configured MCP capabilities, AI can create tasks based on natural-language instructions.

Is MCP limited to ChatGPT or Claude?

No. Chatberry MCP is intended for AI systems that support MCP rather than being limited to a single AI provider.

Does MCP replace Chatberry?

No. MCP provides another way to interact with Chatberry. Your Chatberry workspace, modules and business data remain available through the normal product experience.

Can I control what AI can access?

The capabilities available to an AI system depend on how MCP access and permissions are configured. Businesses should expose only the capabilities they want compatible AI systems to use.
