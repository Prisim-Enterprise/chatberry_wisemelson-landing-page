FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is AI lead qualification?

AI lead qualification uses conversations and relevant lead information to understand a prospect’s needs, collect qualifying details, and help a sales team assess buying intent.

How does Chatberry qualify leads?

Chatberry can engage leads in conversations, ask qualifying questions, collect relevant information, and help identify intent based on responses and configured qualification criteria.

What do high-intent and low-intent tags mean?

High-intent leads show stronger buying signals based on available information. Low-intent leads may need more information or continued nurturing. An intent tag helps prioritise follow-ups; it does not guarantee a purchase.

Can Chatberry tag leads automatically?

Where automatic intent tagging is enabled and supported, Chatberry can categorise leads based on the configured qualification process.

Can AI-qualified leads be sent to a salesperson?

Qualified leads can be handed off or routed to the relevant sales team where the required workflow and integrations are configured.

What analytics can I track?

Depending on the metrics supported in your setup, you can review lead engagement, qualification, intent distribution, handoffs, and conversion outcomes when those outcomes are tracked.

Does a high-intent tag guarantee a sale?

No. It indicates stronger buying signals based on available information. Sales teams should still confirm the lead’s needs and readiness.
