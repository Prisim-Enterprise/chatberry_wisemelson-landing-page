1.Who We Are

Chatberry is offered by Prisim (“Prisim,” “we,” “us,” “our”) as an authorised partner of our technology partner, which develops and hosts the Chatberry platform. Chatberry helps businesses talk to their customers over WhatsApp and other channels, with AI agents, campaigns, ticketing, analytics and related tools (the “Services”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit this website, contact us, or use Chatberry at app.prisim.co.in, and how we use and protect it. We follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

2.Our Role

Website visitors, enquiries and account holders
When you browse our website, message us, or sign up for Chatberry, we decide how your data is used. For this data we are the Data Fiduciary.
Data our customers put into Chatberry
Businesses that use Chatberry upload and manage their own contacts, conversations and files. The business decides what data goes in and why; we process it only on their behalf and on their instructions, as a Data Processor. If you are a customer of a business that uses Chatberry, please contact that business about your data. We will help them answer your request.

3.Information We Collect

a. Information you give us
Your name, business name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, and anything you write to us. This includes the details you share when you ask for a guide, book a demo, join a webinar, apply to become a partner, or chat with us on WhatsApp.
b. Account information
Login details, workspace and team settings, the team members you invite, and billing details for paid plans.
c. Customer content
Contacts, messages, templates, files, call recordings and other data that customers upload to or create in Chatberry. We handle this as described in “Our Role” above.
d. Usage and device data
IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, actions taken in the app, and log and error data. We use this to run, secure and improve the Services.
e. Location data
Only if you use a Chatberry mobile feature that needs it, and only with your explicit permission. See section 6.

4.How We Use Your Information

a. To provide the Services
To set up and run your account, deliver the features you use, and keep the Services working.
b. To answer you
To reply to your enquiries, send the guides you ask for, arrange demos and provide support.
c. To keep things secure
To detect and prevent fraud, spam, abuse and security incidents.
d. To improve Chatberry
To understand how features are used, mostly from aggregated data, so we can fix problems and build better tools.
e. To keep you informed
To send product updates and, where you have agreed, marketing messages. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP on WhatsApp, using the unsubscribe link in an email, or writing to info@prisim.co.in.
f. To meet legal duties
To comply with tax, accounting and other legal requirements, and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not sell your personal data. We use customer content only to provide the Services to the business that uploaded it.

5.WhatsApp and Other Third-Party Services

Our technology partner. The Chatberry platform is built and hosted by our technology partner. When you use Chatberry, your account data and customer content are stored and processed on their platform, on our behalf. They may use only the data they need to run, secure and support the platform, and they are bound by contract to keep it secure and confidential. They also act on behalf of our customers for the customer content those customers upload.

When you contact us over WhatsApp, your message travels through WhatsApp, which is run by Meta. Meta’s own privacy policy covers that part. Chatberry also relies on trusted providers to run the Services, including the WhatsApp Business Platform, cloud hosting, email and SMS delivery, payment processing and AI model providers. We share only the data each provider needs to do its job, and they must protect it under contract.

6.Location Information

Some Chatberry mobile features, such as attendance tracking, field staff updates and location-based tasks, use your device location. We collect it only with your explicit permission and only while those features are in use for your organisation. If you allow background location, Chatberry may read your location while running in the background, only for those features. We never use location data for advertising. You can turn off location permission at any time in your device settings.

7.Sharing and Disclosure

a. Service providers
With our technology partner and the other providers described in section 5, only as needed to run the Services.
b. Your organisation
If your account belongs to a business workspace, the workspace admins can see the data in that workspace.
c. Legal requirements
When the law requires it, or to respond to valid requests from courts, regulators or law enforcement.
d. Business transfers
If Prisim is part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets. Your data stays protected under this policy.
e. With your consent
In any other case, only when you have asked us to or agreed to it.

8.Cookies

Our website uses only the cookies and browser storage it needs to work and to remember your choices. It does not load advertising or third-party analytics trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy and the Cookie Policy in our Terms of Service first.

9.How Long We Keep Data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above. Account data is kept while your account is active. After you close your account, we delete or anonymise it within a reasonable period, unless the law requires us to keep it longer, for example for tax records. Enquiry details are kept as long as needed to answer you and follow up. Customer content is kept and deleted as the customer instructs.

10.Data Security

We protect data with measures such as encryption in transit, access controls, and limiting access to the people who need it. No system can be completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11.Data Stored Outside India

Some of our providers may store or process data outside India. When this happens, we make sure it is permitted by Indian law and that the data stays protected to the standard described here.

12.Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you have the right to:

  • Get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it.
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or updated.
  • Have your data erased when it is no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent you have given us, at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that took place before.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.
  • Have your grievances addressed by us. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, write to info@prisim.co.in.

13.Children

Chatberry is a business service and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us their data, please contact us and we will delete it.

14.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new “Last updated” date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell account holders by email or in the app.
