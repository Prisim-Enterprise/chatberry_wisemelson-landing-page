What is Chatberry Ticketing?

Chatberry Ticketing helps businesses organise customer support requests, assign ownership, track progress, coordinate teams, and manage issues through resolution.

Can customers raise tickets themselves?

Yes. Customers can submit requests through the support pages and forms you set up, and each submission can become a ticket your team can track.

Can I create a customised ticket submission page?

You can set up a customer-facing support page with your branding, the request categories you need and form fields that match your support process. The options available depend on your Chatberry setup.

Can tickets be assigned automatically?

Tickets can be routed using the rules and criteria you configure, such as category or priority, so requests reach the appropriate team or agent.

Can I prioritise urgent tickets?

Yes. Every ticket carries a priority, so your team can see what needs attention first. Where configured, rules can also flag urgent requests or escalate tickets that are close to their deadline.

Can customers receive ticket updates?

Customer notifications and progress updates can be sent through the communication channels supported and configured for your ticket workflow.

Can I track response and resolution times?

Ticket reporting helps teams review response and resolution performance where those metrics are captured. SLA monitoring and overdue alerts depend on the capabilities enabled for your account.

Can different teams manage their own tickets?

Yes. Tickets can be organised around assignment, ownership, categories and team responsibilities, according to how you configure them.

Can I automate ticket follow-ups?

Configured automation can handle supported follow-ups, notifications and escalation conditions, so agents don’t have to track every pending request by hand.

Can I view tickets in different layouts?

Where supported, teams can use different ticket views, such as Kanban, List or Calendar, to organise requests around the way they work.

Is Ticketing suitable for different industries?

Yes. Chatberry Ticketing is built for any business that needs to manage customer issues, service requests and support workflows.
