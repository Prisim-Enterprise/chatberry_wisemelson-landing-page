What is team management software?

Team management software helps organizations organize people, roles, departments, permissions, work assignment, collaboration, and team performance. Chatberry combines these capabilities with conversations, tasks, workflows, and analytics.

Can I create custom roles and permissions?

Yes. Chatberry allows organizations to create roles and define permissions based on responsibilities. Access can be controlled across different areas of the workspace.

Can I manage departments?

Yes. Teams can be organized into departments with their own members, responsibilities, routing rules, workload, and performance views.

What is smart team routing?

Smart team routing automatically assigns conversations, tasks, tickets, or other work based on configured rules such as department, skill, availability, workload, priority, or round-robin distribution.

Does Chatberry support round-robin assignment?

Yes. Round-robin assignment can distribute incoming work across eligible team members. Routing can also consider availability and other configured rules.

Can teams collaborate internally?

Yes. Team members can use internal team conversations while keeping relevant context connected to customer conversations, tasks, tickets, and other work.

Can I see team member activity?

Yes. Team analytics can provide visibility into activity such as conversations handled, tasks completed, tickets resolved, response performance, workload, and active time.

Can I see department performance?

Yes. Managers can analyze work distribution, workload, activity, response performance, and other configured metrics by department.

Does Chatberry support two-factor authentication?

Chatberry can provide two-factor authentication and other configured account-security controls for stronger workspace access protection.

Can I see who changed something?

Chatberry can provide activity and audit information for supported workspace actions, helping administrators understand what changed, who performed the action, and when it happened.

Can Team Management work with Customer OS?

Yes. Customer OS provides customer and business records, while Team Management controls the people, roles, permissions, routing, and access around that work.

Can Team Management work with the Second Brain?

Yes. The Second Brain can help team members remember tasks, follow-ups, commitments, and pending work while Team Management controls ownership, access, departments, and team structure.
