What is omnichannel communication?

Omnichannel communication is an approach that helps businesses manage customer interactions across multiple communication channels in a coordinated way. The goal is to create a more consistent customer experience and help teams manage conversations with better context.

What is the difference between omnichannel and multichannel communication?

Multichannel communication means using multiple channels to reach customers. Omnichannel communication focuses on connecting those channels and the customer experience across them. Simply having several channels does not automatically create an omnichannel experience.

How does Chatberry help businesses manage customer conversations?

Chatberry helps businesses organize customer communication through supported channels and connect conversations with team collaboration, automation, and AI capabilities. The exact experience depends on the channels and features enabled in the business’s setup.

Can businesses manage conversations from multiple channels in one place?

Chatberry is designed to help businesses manage communication through a unified workspace. The specific channels available depend on the integrations and channel support offered by Chatberry.

Can omnichannel communication work with AI Agents?

Omnichannel communication can work alongside AI capabilities. Chatberry’s AI Agents can support suitable customer interactions and configured workflows. Available actions depend on the integrations, permissions, and workflows configured.

Can customer conversations be automated?

Customer communication can be connected to automation workflows to reduce repetitive work and make processes more consistent. The automation available depends on the specific workflow and integrations configured.

Is omnichannel communication suitable for growing businesses?

Yes. A connected communication approach can help growing businesses organize enquiries, coordinate teams, and manage customer interactions as their communication requirements become more complex.
