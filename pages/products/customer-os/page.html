What is a Customer Operating System?

A Customer Operating System is a connected system for managing customer data, interactions, workflows, teams, and business processes. Chatberry’s Customer OS lets organizations create and customize modules, views, workflows, and customer operations around the way their business works.

How is Customer OS different from a CRM?

Traditional CRM software generally provides a predefined structure for managing customers and sales. Customer OS goes further by allowing businesses to create custom modules, relationships, views, workflows, automations, and AI-powered processes around their own operating model.

Can I build a custom CRM with Chatberry?

Yes. Chatberry Customer OS lets organizations create custom modules, fields, relationships, views, statuses, forms, permissions, and workflows. This allows teams to build a customer management system around their own processes.

Can AI create custom modules?

Yes. You can describe the module or process you need using natural language, and Chatberry AI can help create its structure, fields, views, and workflows. You can continue modifying the system as requirements change.

What can I manage with Customer OS?

Customer OS can manage customer records, activities, conversations, journeys, tasks, tickets, campaigns, transactions, and custom business records. The structure can be adapted to the needs of each organization.

Can I customize Customer OS?

Yes. Organizations can customize modules, fields, relationships, views, statuses, forms, permissions, and workflows. AI can also help create and modify parts of the system.

Can Customer OS automate customer workflows?

Yes. Customer OS can connect with Chatberry’s Workflow Orchestration to automate assignments, follow-ups, notifications, record updates, conversations, and other configured business actions.

Can Customer OS connect with other business applications?

Yes. Chatberry supports integrations and connectivity through integrations, APIs, webhooks, and MCP, allowing Customer OS to work with external applications and business systems.

Can different teams use different views of the same data?

Yes. Teams can use different views and permissions while working from the same underlying customer data. This lets each team work according to its own operational needs without duplicating records.

Is Customer OS suitable for different types of businesses?

Yes. Customer OS is designed to be configurable rather than tied to a single business model. Organizations can create the modules, workflows, views, and customer processes relevant to their own operations.
