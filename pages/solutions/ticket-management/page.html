Ticket creation

Every request

Turn customer issues and service requests into trackable tickets. Let customers submit requests through a simple, branded ticket page, while your team can create tickets internally whenever a new issue comes in.

Each ticket can include the details your team needs to act on, such as issue description, request category, customer information, priority, attachments where supported, assigned team or owner, and due date where supported.

Follow-ups and automation

Keep tickets moving,
automatically.

Reduce the need for manual chasing with workflows that trigger actions when a ticket is created, updated, delayed, or resolved.

If a co-working member reports a broken meeting-room display and the ticket remains unresolved past its target time, Chatberry can notify the responsible team and escalate it according to the configured workflow.

  • Automatic assignmentSend tickets to the appropriate team based on defined rules.
  • Follow-up remindersNotify owners when a ticket has been waiting too long.
  • Priority escalationAlert the appropriate manager when a high-priority issue remains unresolved.
  • Status notificationsKeep customers informed when their ticket progresses.
  • Resolution updatesNotify the customer when the issue is resolved and provide next steps where needed.

Example sequence. Triggers, targets and notifications follow the workflow you configure, where supported. Build advanced workflows with Workflow Development

What is a ticket management system?

A ticket management system helps businesses record, assign, track, and resolve customer issues and service requests. It gives each request a clear status, owner, and history.

How does Chatberry Ticket Management work?

Chatberry helps businesses collect requests, create tickets, assign them to teams, track progress, automate follow-ups, and monitor resolution performance through analytics.

Can customers raise tickets through a website?

Businesses can provide a branded ticket submission page where customers can describe an issue and submit a request for the team to handle, subject to the available product configuration.

Can tickets be assigned to different departments?

Tickets can be routed based on configured rules, such as request category, priority, or department, helping each request reach the appropriate team.

Can ticket follow-ups and escalations be automated?

Workflows can be configured to trigger reminders, notifications, and escalations when specified ticket conditions are met, where these actions are supported.

Can I track ticket resolution time and team performance?

Ticket analytics can help teams monitor metrics such as open tickets, overdue tickets, response times, resolution times, and workload by team. The displayed metrics should match the capabilities available in the product.

Can different types of businesses use Chatberry Ticket Management?

Yes. The same ticket management approach can support e-commerce order issues, education requests, co-working facility problems, subscription billing queries, agency change requests, and travel booking assistance.

Can my team manage a ticket from start to finish?

Tickets can be tracked through their configured lifecycle, from creation and assignment through progress updates and final resolution. Activity history can help the team understand what has happened and what needs to happen next, where supported.
