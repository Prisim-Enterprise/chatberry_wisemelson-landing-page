What is business voice calling?

Business voice calling enables companies to make and receive calls for customer enquiries, sales, support, and other business communication.

What is AI voice calling?

AI voice calling uses artificial intelligence to participate in phone conversations, understand spoken requests, respond to customers, and perform supported tasks.

What is the difference between normal voice calling and AI voice calling?

Normal voice calling allows people to communicate directly over the phone. AI voice calling adds an AI agent that can conduct suitable conversations and assist with tasks. Businesses can use either approach depending on their needs and the capabilities available.

Can businesses make and receive calls with Chatberry?

Chatberry Voice Calling is built for inbound and outbound business calls, with call history, duration and outcomes kept in one place. The calling features available to you depend on your calling provider and setup.

Can AI voice agents transfer calls to employees?

Where supported, an AI voice agent can transfer a call to a team member when a conversation needs personal assistance, so the customer reaches the right person.

What analytics can businesses track?

Depending on the calling system, businesses can track call volumes, answered and missed calls, duration, outcomes, and team performance. AI-specific metrics require the relevant AI calling and analytics capabilities.

Can businesses analyse what customers discuss during calls?

Where transcription and conversation insights are enabled, you can see common reasons for calling, frequent questions, recurring issues and follow-ups that are needed.

Does AI voice calling require consent?

Calling requirements depend on the location, purpose, recipient, and type of call. Businesses should follow applicable consent, preference, identification, recording, and opt-out requirements.
