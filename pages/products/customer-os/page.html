04 / Questions FAQ

Goodquestions.

How is Customer OS different from a regular CRM?

A regular CRM gives you a fixed structure. Customer OS lets you define your own modules and views, then connects them to messaging, workflows and AI.

Can different teams see different things?

Yes. Each team can have its own views and permissions while working on the same customer data.

Can I bring in data from other systems?

Yes. Use integrations, APIs and webhooks to keep records in sync with the tools you already run.
