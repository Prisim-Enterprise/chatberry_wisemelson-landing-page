FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Chatberry Webviews

What are Webviews in Chatberry?

Webviews are interactive web experiences that businesses can create for customer-facing tasks, such as product discovery, enquiries, registrations, or bookings. The exact components available depend on Chatberry’s current capabilities.

Can I create Webviews using AI?

Chatberry supports AI-assisted Webview creation according to the capabilities available in the product. Users can create and customise supported experiences and publish them through Chatberry.

Can I publish and host Webviews through Chatberry?

Webviews can be published through Chatberry where the relevant hosting and publishing capabilities are enabled. The published page can then be shared through supported customer touchpoints.

Can I share Webviews in WhatsApp campaigns and chats?

Webviews can be shared through supported WhatsApp campaign and chat workflows. The way the page opens should match the actual WhatsApp experience implemented by Chatberry.

What customer interactions can I track?

Depending on the Webview, product setup, and connected integrations, supported interactions may include page visits, clicks, form submissions, product activity, and conversion events. Only events actually captured by the implementation should be reported.

Can I trigger workflows based on Webview activity?

Supported Webview events can be connected to configured workflows where the relevant tracking, integrations, and actions are available. Messaging follow-ups must also comply with customer permissions and applicable channel policies.

Can I create catalogues, registration pages, and booking experiences?

These are useful Webview use cases. The exact functionality depends on the components and integrations available in Chatberry, such as product data, scheduling, payments, or registration logic.
