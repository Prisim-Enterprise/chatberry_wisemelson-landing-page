How can Chatberry help manufacturing and distribution businesses?

Chatberry helps teams manage customer conversations, collect structured information through webviews and forms, coordinate workflows, support shop floor processes, and review activity through connected insights.

Can we manage customer enquiries through WhatsApp?

Yes. Teams can use WhatsApp conversations to respond to product enquiries, handle dealer and bulk-order requests, share updates, and coordinate follow-ups using the features and workflows configured for their business.

Can customers fill out forms from WhatsApp?

Chatberry webviews can provide a mobile-friendly experience for collecting details such as product requirements, dealer information, quotation requests, and other business inputs.

Can Chatberry support shop floor management?

Chatberry can be configured to coordinate operational workflows such as work orders, shift handovers, maintenance requests, quality checks, and task updates. The exact setup depends on the processes and data sources your business needs.

Can different departments manage their own work?

Chatberry supports team coordination features such as user management, departments, permissions, assignment, and routing, depending on the configuration.

Can AI help answer customer and employee questions?

AI-assisted experiences can help with questions and requests using connected knowledge and configured workflows. Responses and actions depend on the information and integrations available to the system.

Can we track customer and operational performance?

You can review supported campaign, conversation, webview, and operational information through available analytics and connected data. The metrics depend on the events and data sources configured.

Does Chatberry replace our manufacturing ERP or machine-control system?

Chatberry is a connected engagement, workflow, and coordination layer. Existing ERP, inventory, production, or machine systems can remain in place, with integrations considered where available and appropriate.
