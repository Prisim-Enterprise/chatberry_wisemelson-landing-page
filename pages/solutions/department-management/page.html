What is department management software?

Department management software helps businesses organise teams, responsibilities, tools and access. It gives managers a clearer way to coordinate work across the organisation. In Chatberry, that means grouping people into departments, choosing the modules each team uses and managing access from one place.

Can I create my own departments in Chatberry?

Chatberry is designed so you can organise departments around your own structure, such as Marketing, Sales, Operations or Customer Support, or whatever names fit your business. The department settings available depend on your plan and setup.

Can different departments use different Chatberry modules?

Where supported in your setup, each department can work with the modules relevant to it, for example Campaigns for Marketing or Lead Management for Sales. Our team can walk you through the module options available for your workspace.

Can I assign different team members to different departments?

Team members can be organised into the departments they work in using Chatberry’s team management features. How membership is managed depends on your plan and setup.

Can I control what each department can access?

Access is managed through roles, such as owner, manager and team member, so people can reach the modules and information relevant to their work. The exact permission levels available depend on your plan and setup.

Does Chatberry provide ready-made department structures for different industries?

The structures on this page for education, agencies, salons, co-working and subscription businesses are examples to help you plan, not built-in templates. Your departments can be organised however suits your business, and our team can help you plan a structure.

Can business owners view activity across departments?

Owners and managers can review department activity and outstanding work through the information Chatberry makes available in your setup. For deeper performance reporting, see Analytics.

How is Department Management different from Team Collaboration?

Department Management focuses on how an organisation is structured, which people and modules belong to each department, and how access is organised. Team Collaboration focuses on internal communication, task coordination and day-to-day teamwork.
